Pong Game

Game Developer · 2023

PythonPygameSingle Player

Overview

A recreation of the classic arcade game Pong, built with Python and Pygame. The player controls one paddle and faces an opposing CPU that reacts to the ball and defends its side of the court. The goal was to practice building a complete game from scratch, including the game loop, movement, collision detection and scoring.

What I did

I built the whole game on my own: the paddle and ball movement, collision handling, score tracking and the CPU opponent's logic. These were simple introductions into pygame and learning through videos online
