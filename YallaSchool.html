YallaSchool App

FullStack Developer · 2026

Android StudioJavaMaps API

Overview

YallaSchool is a mockup of a school bus app where parents register their children, manage rides to and from school, and pay through a monthly subscription. The goal was to design and build the full parent experience as a working front end, to show how a simpler alternative to calls and paperwork could look and feel.

What I did

I designed and built the entire app on my own. That covered the user experience, the Android front end, and the integration with Maps API for map pathing and location setting. As a mockup, it has no real database or backend storage.
